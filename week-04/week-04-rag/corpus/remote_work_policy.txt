NimbusWorks Inc. — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-009
Last Updated: May 2026

1. Work Arrangements
NimbusWorks operates a hybrid model by default: employees are expected in the office at least 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are the core in-office days for most teams). Fully remote arrangements require director-level approval and are evaluated on a role-by-role basis.

2. Core Hours
All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be online and reachable between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as deliverables are met.

3. Home Office Stipend
Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time $500 home office setup stipend in their first 90 days, plus an ongoing $40/month stipend for internet and utilities. Requests are submitted through the Expensify portal under the "Home Office" category.

4. Working From Abroad
Employees may work from a foreign country for up to 20 business days per year without special approval, provided they notify HR at least 2 weeks in advance for tax and compliance tracking. Longer arrangements require legal and HR review due to tax and visa implications.

5. Equipment for Remote Work
Company laptops, monitors, and headsets ship to the employee's registered home address. Employees are responsible for a secure home network (WPA2/WPA3 encrypted Wi-Fi) and must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems, per the Information Security Policy (IT-SEC-014).

6. Meeting Etiquette
Cameras should be on for team meetings unless there is a stated accommodation. Meetings with any remote participant must have a virtual join option — no "phone in the corner of the room" setups.

7. Performance Expectations
Remote and hybrid employees are held to the same performance standards as in-office employees. Managers are expected to evaluate output and outcomes rather than hours logged or visible activity.

NimbusWorks Inc. — Travel & Expense Policy
Document ID: FIN-POL-003
Last Updated: April 2026

1. General Principles
All business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business. Employees should book the most cost-effective option available that meets business needs. Personal expenses must never be submitted, even accidentally mixed with business expenses.

2. Expense Submission
All expenses must be submitted via the Expensify portal within 30 days of being incurred. Each expense requires an itemized receipt for amounts over $25. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the transaction date will not be reimbursed except with VP-level approval.

3. Travel Booking
Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance when possible, in economy class for flights under 6 hours. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Hotel bookings should not exceed $250/night in major cities ($180/night elsewhere) without prior approval.

4. Meals
Daily meal reimbursement while traveling is capped at $75/day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $30 dinner). Client meals are reimbursable at reasonable cost but require the client's name and company to be noted in the expense report. Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment, up to $50 per event, and requires manager approval.

5. Ground Transportation
Rideshare and taxi expenses are reimbursable for business travel. Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts.

6. Approval Thresholds
Expenses under $500 require manager approval only. Expenses between $500 and $2,000 require manager and department head approval. Expenses over $2,000 require VP approval and must be pre-approved before the expense is incurred.

7. Non-Reimbursable Items
The company does not reimburse: personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, airline seat upgrades beyond premium economy, spa or gym services, or lost personal property.

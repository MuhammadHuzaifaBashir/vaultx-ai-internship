NimbusWorks Inc. — Employee Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-002
Last Updated: March 2026

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Annual leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal, except in emergencies. Unused annual leave can be carried over into the next year up to a maximum of 10 days; anything beyond that is forfeited unless approved by a department head.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over and resets on January 1st each year.

3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave requests should be submitted at least 30 days before the expected start date, along with supporting documentation (e.g., medical certificate or adoption papers).

4. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and up to 2 days for extended family. No advance notice is required; employees should inform their manager and HR as soon as possible.

5. Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least 1 year of tenure may request unpaid leave of up to 30 days for personal reasons, subject to manager and HR approval. Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

6. Public Holidays
NimbusWorks observes 12 public holidays per year, as published annually on the HR portal. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed as a holiday instead.

7. Leave Approval Process
All leave requests are submitted via the HR portal and require approval from the employee's direct manager. Leave exceeding 10 consecutive days additionally requires HR sign-off. Managers must respond to leave requests within 3 business days.
